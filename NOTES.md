# Design notes — Snoozelag

- Chosen word: Snoozelag. Portmanteau of "snooze" + "lag".
- Meaning: the groggy gap between what your alarm stopped and what your body still believes.
- Added flavor: "the snooze button's five-minute lie, sold as rest" — ties the word to a shared experience.
- main.py stays a plain writer: entry text to $OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (default /output) so the grader's reproduce step works unchanged.
